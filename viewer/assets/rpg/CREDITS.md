# Generic RPG Pack

Pixel art by Bakudas (https://twitter.com/bakudas) and Gabe Fern (https://twitter.com/_Gabrielfer),
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). Obtained from the Bevy engine repository
(bevyengine/bevy, `assets/textures/rpg`, see its CREDITS.md).

The viewer recolours these sprites at load time (dirt to sand, tunics to faction colours, canopies to
green and autumn, winter whitening). No attribution is required by the licence; it is given anyway.
